## Sounds
- `sfx-library/kit/` — CC0 (public domain): [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) packs and hand-checked [Freesound](https://freesound.org) recordings; per-sound credits are in each `.json` sidecar and in `kit/CREDITS.md` after download.
- `sfx-library/extra/` — CC0 recordings from Freesound found via [Openverse](https://openverse.org); author and source URL for every file are in `extra/manifest.json`.
- `sfx-library/favorites-pack/` — copies of the curated favourites from the two sets above (same licences).
- The four Remotion meme effects used in the example reel (whoosh, vine boom, record scratch, …) come from `https://remotion.media/` and are not redistributed here.
